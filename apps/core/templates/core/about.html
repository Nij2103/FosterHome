About the System

Our Mission

Putting Children First Through Better Data

The Foster Care Placement Predictor was developed to address one of the most challenging problems in child welfare: matching children with the right foster families. Placement disruptions — where a child must return to care because a placement breaks down — have lasting negative effects on child development.

By applying machine learning to historical placement data, we help case workers identify the most compatible placements earlier, reducing disruption risk and improving long-term outcomes for children and families alike.

Important: All data in this system is synthetically generated for demonstration purposes. No real child or family records are stored. Prediction scores are decision-support tools, not placement decisions — they must be reviewed by qualified case workers.

What We Aim to Achieve

System Objectives

Reduce Disruptions

Use predictive modeling to identify high-risk placements before they are formalized, giving case workers early warning signals.

Support Case Workers

Provide professionals with data-driven insights that complement — not replace — their expertise and relationships with families.

Ensure Privacy

All training data is synthetic. Role-based access control ensures only authorized staff can view or modify sensitive records.

Enable Compliance

Full audit trails and model version tracking ensure the system meets regulatory and compliance needs.
