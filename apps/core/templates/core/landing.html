What We Offer

A Complete Placement Management Platform

Placement Matching

Our machine learning models analyze child and family profile completion data to generate a percentage compatibility score — giving case workers an objective, data-informed recommendation to support confident placement decisions.

Child & Family Registry

A centralized registry of all children and approved foster families. Full profile management with structured assessment fields, search, filtering, and profile completion tracking to ensure prediction-ready records.

Analytics & Reporting

A live analytics dashboard with in-memory SVG charts covering child age distribution, family capacity, compatibility scores, placement outcomes, and monthly placement volume trends.

Placement Tracking

Record and track every placement across its full lifecycle — proposed, active, completed, or disrupted — with start/end dates, disruption reasons, and timestamped audit records for compliance.

Role-Based Access Control

Three distinct roles — Administrator, Case Worker, and Viewer — ensure sensitive records are only modified by authorized personnel, while Viewers can access dashboards, analytics, and placement reports in read-only mode.

Export & Interoperability

A versioned REST API allows external systems to query child, family, placement, and prediction data programmatically for integration with existing case management workflows.

The Process

How the System Works

1

Register Children & Families

Administrators and Case Workers register child profiles (age, special needs, sibling group, language, behavioral and mental health support levels) and foster family profiles (capacity, languages, household composition, preferred age group) and complete the assessment sections to reach 100% profile completion.

2

Request an ML Prediction

Once both profiles are 100% complete, select a child and a candidate family. The system automatically extracts features and runs the trained Random Forest model to return a percentage compatibility score with a full prediction breakdown.

3

Confirm the Placement

After reviewing the compatibility score, the case worker creates a formal placement record. The placement is tracked through its full lifecycle — proposed, active, completed, or disrupted — and appears in the analytics dashboard and compliance reports.
