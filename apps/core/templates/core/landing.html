What We Offer

A Complete Placement Management Platform

Every tool a case worker needs — from child intake to family matching, ML-driven predictions, and comprehensive reporting.

AI Placement Matching

Our machine learning models analyze child characteristics and family profiles to generate a compatibility score — giving case workers an objective, data-informed recommendation alongside their professional judgement.

Child & Family Registry

A centralized registry of all children in the care system and approved foster families. Full CRUD management with search, filtering, and role-based access so only authorized staff can modify records.

Analytics & Reporting

A real-time dashboard, 20+ interactive charts covering age distribution, placement outcomes, model performance, and state-wise trends — plus exportable PDF and CSV reports for stakeholders.

Placement Tracking

Record and track every placement — from "proposed" through "active", "completed", and if necessary, "disrupted" — with start/end dates, disruption reasons, and a full audit trail for compliance.

Role-Based Access Control

Three distinct roles — Administrator, Case Worker, and Viewer — ensure that sensitive records are only modified by authorized personnel, while read-only stakeholders can still access dashboards and reports.

Export & Interoperability

Export any dataset or report as CSV, Excel, or PDF at any time. A versioned REST API allows external systems to query placement data and trigger prediction requests programmatically.

The Process

How the System Works

1

Register Children & Families

Case workers add child profiles (age, special needs, sibling group, behavioral index) and foster family profiles (capacity, experience, home type, accepted needs) into the secure registry.

2

Request an ML Prediction

Select a child and a candidate family. The system runs the trained machine learning model and returns a 0–1 compatibility score with a full breakdown, exportable as a PDF report.

3

Confirm the Placement

After reviewing the prediction alongside other case factors, the case worker creates a formal placement record that is tracked through its full lifecycle and included in compliance reports.
